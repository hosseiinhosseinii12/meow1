Help

Questions, answered

Add your chosen pieces to your bag and proceed to checkout. You may check out as a guest or create an account to save your details, track orders and access your wishlist from any device.

Orders may be amended or cancelled within two hours of placement from your account, or by contacting our concierge. After that, pieces enter preparation in our atelier and can be returned once received.

Every order arrives in our signature linen box. Complimentary gift wrapping with a handwritten card can be selected at checkout.

We offer complimentary standard shipping on all orders over $250 worldwide. Express and next-day services are available at checkout.

Standard delivery takes three to five working days, express two working days, and next-day delivery is available in selected cities when ordered before 2pm.

We deliver to more than 90 countries. Duties and taxes are calculated at checkout so there are no surprises on arrival.

You may return unworn pieces in their original condition within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Return shipping is complimentary.

Visit your order in your account and select Request a Return. We will email a prepaid label and, in many cities, can arrange a courier collection.

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within five working days of your return arriving at our atelier.

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay and, in selected regions, cash on delivery.

All payments are encrypted and processed by certified payment partners. We never store full card details on our servers.

Interest-free instalments are available on orders over $500 in selected regions at checkout.

Select Forgot Password on the sign-in page and we will email a secure link valid for one hour.

Yes, your account can hold as many delivery addresses as you need, with one set as your default.

Every email includes an unsubscribe link, or you may manage preferences under Notifications in your account.

Each product page includes a size guide with precise garment measurements. Our concierge is also happy to advise by phone or chat.

We work only with tanneries holding Leather Working Group Gold certification, mills that trace their fibre to the farm, and suppliers we visit in person every year.

Every piece is covered by our lifetime repair service. Contact concierge and we will arrange collection.
