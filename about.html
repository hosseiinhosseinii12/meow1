Our story

Fifteen years, three ateliers

What began as a room of shirts has become a house of makers. We still work with the first three workshops we ever met.

  1. 2011

    A single room in Marylebone

    Charlotte Vale opens a small shop selling shirts from three Portuguese workshops, and a handful of shoes from Northampton.

  2. 2014

    The first signature coat

    Our double-faced cashmere coat is made with a family mill in Biella. Two hundred are sold before winter ends.

  3. 2018

    Florence

    We open our own leather workshop in the Oltrarno, where every Aldwych tote is now cut and stitched by hand.

  4. 2022

    Lifetime repairs

    We promise to repair everything we sell, forever, by the people who made it. Over nine thousand pieces have come home since.

  5. 2026

    Kyoto

    A third atelier opens in a restored machiya, devoted to horology and eyewear.

What we hold to

By hand

Everything we sell is finished by a person, not a process. We name the artisan on every care card.

Responsibly

Leather Working Group Gold tanneries, traceable fibre and mills we visit in person every year.

For decades

We design for the tenth year of wear, not the first. Then we repair what time does to it.

With care

A personal concierge for every client, and an answer within the hour, six days a week.
